# Design doc / RFC template (the learner writes into `docs/rfcs/`)

1. **Title, author, status, date.**
2. **Context and problem:** the business pain, who is affected, how we know (data).
3. **Goals and non-goals.**
4. **Options considered (at least two):** for each, how it works, costs, risks.
5. **Proposal:** the design, with a diagram, data model, API changes, failure modes.
6. **Rollout and rollback plan.**
7. **Observability and SLO impact.**
8. **Security and privacy.**
9. **Cost and capacity.**
10. **Open questions and what would make us reverse this.**
11. **ADR links** for any default stack change.
