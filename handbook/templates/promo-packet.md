# Promotion packet (the learner writes this; Claude does not)

Copy to `learner/promo-packet.md`.

1. **Target level and date.**
2. **Scope and impact:** what you delivered since the last level, in business terms, with links (PRs, issues, deploys).
3. **Coverage table:** for each required subcode, two or more evidence links from two or more sprints, and one sentence on why each shows the behaviour.
4. **Trend:** first-pass approval, review rounds, recurring findings, hint use, with your reading of them.
5. **Hardest problem:** what was hard, what you tried, what you learned, and what you would do differently.
6. **Reviewing and mentoring** (L3+): reviews you gave and what changed because of them.
7. **Gaps I know about:** be honest. The committee will check.
8. **Exam result and links.**
